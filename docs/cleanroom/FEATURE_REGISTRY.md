Status vocabulary:

- **verified** - Physically exercised on the named hardware; all acceptance gates met.
- **implemented_unverified** - Code and tests exist, but physical verification is outstanding.
- **blocked** - Reachability is prevented by a named blocker with evidence.
- **out_of_scope** - Excluded with explicit product-owner approval recorded.
- **unimplemented** - Targeted, not built. No claim is made.

A row may be unimplemented: it is targeted work, not a claim. The
release gate requires no unimplemented rows remain; at that point every
row is verified, blocked, or out_of_scope with recorded approval.

## Blocked rows and why

- foundation.privilege - [B-HCI-OWNERSHIP](BLOCKERS.md#b-hci-ownership): K2a. The A52 chain gives init-domain uid 0 but has not bound HCI_CHANNEL_USER under enforcing SELinux.
- foundation.broker - [B-APP-AGENT-BROKER](BLOCKERS.md#b-app-agent-broker): Measured: shell domain connects to @dfi_init, app-domain runas_app gets EACCES; the relay is unauthenticated raw-syscall root and never returns to init.
- foundation.controller-owner - [B-HCI-OWNERSHIP](BLOCKERS.md#b-hci-ownership): The iPhone Bluetooth stack is the controller owner and exposes link control to the Watch app.
- pair.optical - [B-OPTICAL-FIRMWARE](BLOCKERS.md#b-optical-firmware): No lawful independent decoder exists in the public tree; Apple VisualPairing firmware is absent and not vendorable.
- comm.walkie-talkie - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The iPhone hosts Walkie-Talkie sessions between paired watches.
- media.home-controls - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): Apple Home accessory control is account- and first-party-app bound; a local HAP bridge would not appear in the Watch Home app.
- faces.native-resources - [B-ASSET-APPLERENDER](BLOCKERS.md#b-asset-applerender): Apple-rendered face resources are not redistributable. An original renderer is a separate, unblocked effort.
- apps.siri - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The Watch invokes Siri through the paired iPhone and Apple account services.
- apps.install - [B-WATCH-APP-SIGNING](BLOCKERS.md#b-watch-app-signing): The iPhone installs a compatible iPhone app's Watch extension.
- apps.update - [B-WATCH-APP-SIGNING](BLOCKERS.md#b-watch-app-signing): The iPhone updates installed Watch apps.
- apps.watchos-update - [B-WATCHOS-UPDATE](BLOCKERS.md#b-watchos-update): The iPhone downloads and verifies a watchOS update.
- apps.watchos-update-install - [B-WATCHOS-UPDATE](BLOCKERS.md#b-watchos-update): The iPhone installs a watchOS update and reports progress.
- apps.store-purchase - [B-APP-STORE-DRM](BLOCKERS.md#b-app-store-drm): The Watch App Store can purchase apps with the Apple account.
- apps.drm-playback - [B-APP-STORE-DRM](BLOCKERS.md#b-app-store-drm): The Watch plays protected media authorised by the account.
- apps.third-party-complications - [B-WATCH-APP-SIGNING](BLOCKERS.md#b-watch-app-signing): The iPhone installs app complications on the Watch.
- secure.apple-id - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The iPhone holds the owner's Apple ID and passes the authorised account state to the Watch.
- secure.icloud-sync - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The iPhone syncs Watch-scoped data through iCloud.
- secure.activation-lock - [B-ACTIVATION-LOCK](BLOCKERS.md#b-activation-lock): The iPhone can show whether Activation Lock is enabled for the Watch.
- secure.activation-lock-bypass - [B-ACTIVATION-LOCK](BLOCKERS.md#b-activation-lock): Refused by policy, never implemented. Listed so the refusal is auditable.
- secure.apple-pay - [B-APPLE-PAY](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-pay): The iPhone provisions and manages Apple Pay cards for the Watch.
- secure.wallet - [B-APPLE-PAY](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-pay): The iPhone adds and manages passes shown on the Watch.
- secure.transit - [B-APPLE-PAY](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-pay): The iPhone provisions transit cards and express travel state.
- secure.secure-element - [B-APPLE-PAY](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-pay): The iPhone provisions Apple-controlled applets into the Watch secure element.
- secure.cellular-provisioning - [B-CELLULAR-PROVISIONING](BLOCKERS.md#b-cellular-provisioning): The iPhone activates a Watch eSIM with the carrier.
- secure.appstore - [B-APP-STORE-DRM](BLOCKERS.md#b-app-store-drm): The iPhone authorises Watch App Store account access.
- secure.imessage - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The iPhone relays iMessage conversations to the Watch.
- secure.facetime - [B-APPLE-ACCOUNT](BLOCKERS.md#b-apple-account): The iPhone relays FaceTime calls to the Watch.
