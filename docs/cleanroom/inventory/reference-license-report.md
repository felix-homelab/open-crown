# Reference licence position

**Subject:** the quarantined copy at `reference/apple_watch_connector/` (reference project: "Apple Watch Companion", Java/Flutter/Rust, four host platforms).
**Question:** what may Open Crown lawfully take from it, and what does the copy's own licence evidence actually say?
**Answer in one line:** the repository has **no licence at all**, so its contents are all rights reserved; the only licence evidence in the tree covers three AOSP-derived interface files and some tooling, and the project's own documents admit that permission to redistribute its Apple-derived assets is unresolved.

## 1. Licence-file census

| File | Present? | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `LICENSE` / `LICENCE` / `COPYING` at repo root | **No** | — |
| `LICENSE` in any subproject (`apple-watch-bridge/`, `apple-watch-companion/`, `crates/`, `linux-watch-host/`, `windows-watch-host/`) | **No** | — |
| `NOTICE` | **Yes, one:** `apple-watch-bridge/NOTICE` (17 lines) | AOSP AIDL derivation (Apache-2.0); a statement that a neighbouring project was inspected read-only |
| `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`, SBOM, `licenses/` directory | **No** | — |
| SPDX identifiers or licence headers in `README.md` | **No** | — |
| `docs/DONATIONS.md` | Present, but it is a funding page, not a licence | — |

The only licence-bearing statement in the README is the closing paragraph of the English section (line 1586, mirrored in Russian at line 3126). It says, in effect, that third-party licences and provenance stay in their own files and reports, points at the bridge NOTICE for its components, and declines to attach a new blanket licence to that material.

That is a refusal to grant, not a grant. It also points to files that mostly do not exist in the copy.

## 2. Consequence of having no licence

- Default copyright applies: the author retains all rights; copying, modification, and distribution are not permitted absent a licence or a statutory exception.
- There is no "open source" position to rely on, no patent grant, no trademark licence, and no warranty disclaimer.
- Facts and ideas are not protected by copyright, so reading the prose and writing our own specification remains permissible under our own policy (`docs/03-clean-room.md` §3). Expression — code, comments, identifier names, string tables, assets, document text — is protected and must not be copied.
- The reference's prose documents are themselves copyrightable. This audit paraphrases and cites; it does not reproduce their text.

## 4. Apple-origin material and Apple's terms

The tree contains a large amount of Apple-derived material without any Apple licence:

1. **Renderer outputs.** Face previews, shared pixel maps, pigment swatches, component programs, family thumbnails (182,432,179 bytes / 20,128 files) produced by hooking Apple's face compositors inside a simulator, plus catalogue JSON keyed to a specific watchOS build. The reference describes the process openly in `docs/WATCH_APP_NATIVE_OPTION_SWATCHES_90.md` and `docs/WATCH_FACES_NATIVE_COVERAGE_51.md`.
2. **Apple-format face packages.** Four `.watchface` archives in test resources, including one real-sized package (130,885 bytes).
3. **Protocol data derived from Apple binaries.** NPS field numbers, route names, message counters, header layouts and error codes, reconstructed by disassembly. The reference itself warns that addresses are build-specific (`docs/live-20261005-sync/SETUP_CHAIN.md` "Evidence boundaries").
4. **Absent firmware inputs.** The camera decoder requires an Apple `VisualPairing` firmware image and a private generation script; both are excluded by `.gitignore` (`/firmware/`, `/research-tools/`) and are not in the copy. The reference's build guide calls this "an open release gate" (`docs/BUILD_AND_RELEASE.md` "Companion inputs and distribution keys").

The project's own statements on redistribution:

- `docs/BUILD_AND_RELEASE.md`, "Remaining release gates": the guide lists three items that must be settled before anything is published — dedicated signing, dependency notices, and **permission to ship assets derived from firmware**.
- `docs/REFACTOR_RELEASE_92.md` line 116: "**redistribution permissions for firmware-derived assets remain unresolved**."
- `docs/REFACTOR_RELEASE_93.md` line 88 lists "notices and native-asset redistribution" among open items.
- `docs/live-20261006-optical-pairing/RESULT.md` describes the decoder as "not … a generally redistributable decoder".

That is an admission by the reference that its own asset distribution is not cleared. It cannot transfer a permission it does not have.

Additionally:

- **No trademark notice.** No statement of non-affiliation with Apple, no attribution of Apple marks. "Apple Watch" and Apple framework names are used descriptively.
- **No patent analysis.** The protocol originates with Apple; patents may read on it. Nothing in the reference addresses this.
- **No Apple SDK licence text.** Apple's SDK/simulator terms are not reproduced, and the project does not explain how simulator-derived renders are licensed for redistribution.
- **Personal data.** `docs/DONATIONS.md` contains a live cryptocurrency address; several reports contain device identifiers, pair UUIDs, and home-network details. None of it may be carried into Open Crown.

## 5. What this means for Open Crown

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| Code, comments, identifiers, file names, strings, generated code | Prohibited. No licence; copying would infringe. Our `docs/03-clean-room.md` already forbids it. |
| Protocol facts (field numbers, routes, state ordering, limits) | Usable as ideas/facts; must be re-expressed in our vocabulary and, where possible, confirmed by our own captures. Facts reconstructed from Apple binaries should be treated as *hypotheses* until captured. |
| Apple-derived assets (previews, swatches, catalogues, `.watchface`) | Prohibited. Do not vendor, mirror, or "re-generate identically". If previews are a product requirement, Open Crown must render them itself from its own geometry or from the user's own device at runtime. |
| AOSP AIDL | Allowed under Apache-2.0, but take it from AOSP upstream with its NOTICE, never from the reference. |
| Gradle wrapper / Flutter template art | Allowed under their upstream licences; obtain from upstream. |
| Documentation artwork | No licence → do not copy; commission or generate our own. |
| Dependency choices | We may depend on the same upstream packages, but we must publish our own SBOM and licence texts (`docs/03-clean-room.md` §8). |

Interaction with our own licensing:

- Our MIT licence (`LICENSE`, ADR 0006) covers only our code. It cannot launder Apple-derived assets or third-party notices into compliance; MIT also grants no patent licence, which ADR 0006 already records as accepted risk.
- MIT does not require us to track the reference at all. Provenance discipline is a separate, voluntary commitment; this audit exists to make it auditable.

## 6. Concrete prohibitions derived from this report

1. Do not add the reference tree, or any file from it, to the Open Crown repository, build, test fixtures, CI cache, or release artifact.
2. Do not copy its README/docs text. Cite section names; paraphrase.
3. Do not reproduce its assets, including "just a preview" or "just a swatch".
4. Do not require Apple firmware, an Apple IPSW, a dyld cache, or an extracted decoder in any Open Crown build.
5. Do not use its package namespace, class names, or wire command strings (see `similarity-plan.md`).
6. Do not present its claims as evidence. Several of its own cited evidence files are absent from the copy (see `reference-features.md` §5).
